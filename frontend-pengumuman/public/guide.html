Example 3 🔄 Substitute Teacher (Inval)

Substituting for an Absent Colleague

Scenario: Ms. Endah is on sick leave. Mr. Dion covers her post at 2nd Floor Canteen.

  1. Mr. Dion scans the 1 QR Code in the Canteen.

Step-by-Step Check-In Procedure

1
Arrive at Your Duty Post 2-3 Minutes Early

Walk directly to your designated area (Canteen, Lobby, or ESE Backyard). Find the printed QR code on the wall or desk.

2
Scan the QR Code with Your Phone Camera

Open your phone camera or QR scanner. Tap the link that appears: https://pengumuman.klprojects.online/duty/...

3
Select Scheduled Teacher Name

Select the name of the teacher scheduled for duty. If you are covering for an absent colleague, select their name first.

4
🔄 Are You a Substitute (Inval)?

If you are replacing an absent teacher, select your own name in the "🔄 Guru Pengganti / Inval" dropdown. Then enter your own 5-digit PIN. The system records your attendance while crediting the substitution.

5
Enter Your Secret 5-Digit PIN & Submit

Type your permanent 5-digit PIN and tap "Submit Attendance". Look for the green confirmation banner with your exact timestamp.

Teacher Help & Frequently Asked Questions

Simple answers to common questions about duty check-in and system policies.

❓ Why is there only 1 QR code at the Canteen now?

To prevent confusion with multiple printed papers. Whether you are on Lantai 1 or Lantai 2, scan the single Canteen QR code on the wall. Your phone will show the session selector to pick your grade and floor.

❓ Why does the system refuse check-in 10 minutes early?

Under the Strict Zero-Tolerance Policy, teacher presence is tracked when students are actively in the duty zone. The check-in unlocks right on schedule (e.g. 07:15 for morning duty). Please arrive 2-3 minutes beforehand.

❓ What should I do if school Wi-Fi is slow or disconnects?

Switch your mobile phone to cellular data (4G/5G). The attendance page is ultra-lightweight (less than 25 KB) and transmits in under 1 second even on weak mobile signals.

❓ What if I forget my 5-digit PIN?

Your 5-digit PIN is permanently assigned to your teacher account. Contact the Duty Coordinator or school administration to quickly look up or reset your passcode.

❓ How do I ask the AI Chatbot about my duty schedule?

Open the chat window on the web portal and type: "Jadwal piket [Your Name]" or "Siapa yang piket di kantin hari Kamis?". The AI assistant responds instantly with your real-time duties.

❓ Can I check in from my classroom or staff room?

No. Attendance must strictly be completed on-site at your duty post (Canteen, Lobby, Backyard) during duty hours to ensure student supervision.
